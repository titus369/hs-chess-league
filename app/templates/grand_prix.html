Individual grand prix

Points from the Lichess arenas held alongside the league. Weekly arenas pay 20-12-8-6-4 to the top 5. The kickoff arena pays 30-18-12-9-6-5-4-3-2-1 to the top 10, plus 5-3-2 to every member of the top 3 Lichess teams who played at least one game. Everyone who played at least one game in an arena is listed, even with 0 points. Ties are broken by total arena points (the points scored inside the Lichess arenas). Players are matched to the league by Lichess username.
