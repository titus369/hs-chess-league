Player lineups

Results are home–away. F marks a forfeit: 1F-0F means the away player didn't show, 0F-1F the home player didn't, 0F-0F neither. A player box can be left empty (--) for a side that forfeits or until the lineup is known.

To fill a table from Google Sheets, copy its 4 board rows (Home name, Home username, Home rating, Result, Away name, Away username, Away rating; a leading board-number column and a header row are fine), paste them into that table's box, click Fill table, check the boxes, then save. Players are matched to the roster by Lichess username. Anyone not on the roster is shown as “New substitute” and is added to the team as a substitute when you save, if they're eligible.
